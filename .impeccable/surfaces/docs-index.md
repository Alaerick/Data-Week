# Surface brief: docs/index.html ("Closing In on 1.5")

Mode: Read. Build path: code-led (Read surface; no image generation).
Audience and job: competition judges grasp the 1.5 claim on the first screen and verify it in the charts and methods (see PRODUCT.md).
Must stay: the author's prose, blue = 2-point / orange = 3-point color code, chart behavior, light/dark following the device.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is framed by a painted half court, because the 3-point arc is literally the line where a two becomes a three. It refuses the neutral data-journalism template (off-white ground, eyebrow label, bold system-sans headline).

OWN-WORLD: Painted-court navy field with chalk-white court lines; inside the arc tinted toward the 2-point blue, beyond it toward the 3-point orange. Reading column on a cool chalk ground (never cream). Archivo, widened, for display and section heads; Public Sans for reading and tabular numbers. Section breaks drawn as thin court lines.

STORY: The reader sees the arc split twos from threes, reads the claim, learns the 1.5 rule with the sliders, sees that balance wins, watches the league close in, and checks the method.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width navy court band. Title, subtitle and context line at the top. Beneath them a 3D half court seen from mid-court: every made shot of 2025-26 (blue twos, orange threes) flies to the basket in date order and lands as a dot, so the floor fills in with the season's shot map; the camera drifts slowly. No text or controls in the animation. The flat SVG court remains as the fallback.

FORM: Court diagram, position 1 of the ordered list. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
